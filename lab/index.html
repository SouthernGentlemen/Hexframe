Hexframe combat laboratory

A browser-playable fighting-game prototype built around deterministic 60 Hz simulation, authored hitboxes, rollback-ready state, and build crafting. The interactive client requires JavaScript; no sign-in is required at /play/.

Keyboard controls

  • Move with A/D or the left and right arrow keys.
  • Jump with W, Space, or the up arrow key; crouch with S or the down arrow key.
  • Use light, medium, and heavy attacks with J, K, and L.
  • Use the special action with I and guard with U.

Current systems

The playtest includes a 16-slot move set, five armor slots, white through orange armor grades, flat armor and elemental resistances, threshold-based armor skills, a split equipment/material inventory, and armor crafting.
