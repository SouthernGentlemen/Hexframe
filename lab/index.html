Hexframe

A browser-playable fighting-game prototype built around deterministic 60 Hz simulation, authored hitboxes, rollback-ready state, and build crafting. The interactive game requires JavaScript; no sign-in is required.

Keyboard controls

  • Move with WASD and attack with the arrow-key diamond.
  • Hold Shift for Setup, Ctrl/Command for Power, or both for Finale techniques.
  • Interact with doors, shrines, chests, and forges using E.
  • Open the systems menu with Escape.

Current systems

The playtest includes a 16-slot move set, five armor slots, white through orange armor grades, flat armor and elemental resistances, threshold-based armor skills, a split equipment/material inventory, and armor crafting.
