# Hexframe v0.7.6

**Product:** Hexframe  
**Version:** v0.7.6  
**Release date:** 2026-08-31  
**Commit:** the commit this tag names — `git rev-list -n1 v0.7.6`

## Scope

Prevent touch-only visitors from entering Hexframe training without understanding its input
requirements. The public training entry now identifies coarse-pointer devices and presents a
clear keyboard-or-gamepad requirement before tutorial or free practice begins.

## Included changes

- [HF-106] [FIX] Warn touch users about training controls
- [HF-107] [BUILD] Record and version the v0.7.6 release

## Accessibility boundary

- The notice is present in semantic markup with `role="note"`.
- A coarse primary pointer reveals the notice without relying on viewport width.
- Fine-pointer desktop layouts and existing keyboard/gamepad training behavior are unchanged.
- The notice does not claim that a paired gamepad is unsupported on a touch device.

## Known limitations

- Hexframe does not yet provide on-screen combat controls. Touch-only visitors receive an
  explanation rather than a virtual control pad.
- Browsers do not expose a reliable general-purpose test for the presence of a physical
  keyboard, so the notice uses the primary-pointer capability as the mobile signal.
