## Scope

Make Hexframe's public surface lead with the playable training lab. The overview and
training entry now show the real stage, player, and dummy instead of an abstract graphic
or loadout chooser. Public training opens directly into tutorial or free practice.

## Public surface

- `/` introduces the project through the playable training workflow.
- `/play/` shows the real stage, player, and dummy with direct tutorial and free-practice actions.
- Public training no longer asks the player to choose a loadout.
- The JavaScript mount stays empty until rendering begins, preventing fallback copy from flashing.
- Free practice uses the same stage and deterministic combat simulation as the developer lab.
