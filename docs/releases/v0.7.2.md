**Product:** Hexframe

## Scope

Refocus Hexframe on the deterministic developer laboratory and restore the animated Move
Codex as a separate authenticated developer surface. Black Belfry and the campaign remain
implemented source history, but they are no longer the default Hexframe presentation.

## Resulting public surface

- `/` enters the protected developer laboratory.
- `/lab` remains the authenticated entry to the Training Grid with developer tools.
- The lab foregrounds frame transport, authoritative state inspection, geometry overlays,
  interaction history, deterministic captures, replay verification, and frame timelines.
- `/codex/` and `/codex/moves/:id/` are authenticated developer routes with live move
  demonstrations, Demo/Hit/Block modes, playback speed, pause/step/scrub controls, frame
  timelines, search, and move detail.
- The Codex does not construct Black Belfry campaign or stage content.
- Campaign/Fight/player routes remain implemented for provenance and direct testing but no
  longer define the default Hexframe experience.

## Redirect correction

Redirect responses involved in lab entry, Training developer-tool stripping, sign-in, and
sign-out intentionally carry no text body. This removes the transient redirect text that
was visibly flashing during navigation while preserving the existing status codes,
locations, cookies, and authentication controls.
