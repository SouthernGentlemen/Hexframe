## Deployment

Deploys to `hexframe.wizardgang.ai` as a **new Cloudflare Worker on a new origin**.

**Player saves from the former `hexframe.wizardgang.ai` deployment are not transferred.**
Browser storage and the identity cookie are origin-scoped, and the durable save objects
belong to the previous Worker. This is a deliberate pre-1.0 decision, recorded here rather
than left implicit. Players starting on Hexframe begin with a new save.

## Known limitations

- Network play is not implemented.
- The roster is limited to the reference fighter and the Bell Warden.
- Commits and tags are unsigned until a signing key is configured for the repository.
